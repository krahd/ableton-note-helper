History Through CU

The world’s history and CU’s history are one history.

Four dates demonstrate four different historical relations. The timeline is an entry point; opening a date enters a guided multimedia journey built from attributable material.

The project keeps sources visible and preserves the distinction between documented relation and temporal proximity.

Sources and media rights

Media and claims remain attributable.

Reusable media is integrated directly. Other archival material remains source-linked when reuse rights are uncertain or item-specific.
