About the prototype

One historical field, not two parallel stories.

This prototype tests a central proposition: the history of the world and the history of CU are not separate histories. The interface places wider historical events and contemporaneous CU activity around the same temporal spine.

Juxtaposition does not imply causality. Some CU materials directly respond to a wider event; others simply show what the institution and its communities were doing at the same time.

Visible historical claims are linked to verified public sources. The funded project would expand the corpus, primary-source integration, accessibility, archival partnerships and historical coverage.
